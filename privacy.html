Privacy policy

We collect as little as we can, we don’t track you, and we don’t sell anything to anyone. This page says exactly what happens to the small amount of information the site does handle.

Last updated 13 September 2026.

Who we are

Grown and Made UK CIC is the data controller for this website. We’re a community interest company limited by guarantee, registered in England and Wales, company number 17415545, registered office Unit A, 82 James Carter Road, Mildenhall, Bury St Edmunds IP28 7DE. You can reach us at hello@grownandmade.uk.

What we collect, and why

When you suggest a business. The suggestion form asks about the business — its name, website, address, and what you know about where its products are made — and it asks for your name, email address and your connection to it, so we can tell an owner from an enthusiastic customer and come back to you if we need to ask something. Your suggestion goes into a private review queue that only we can open, and we work through it there. It is never published, and your name and email never appear on the map.

When you update your own listing. Businesses we have listed are sent a private link to a page showing everything we hold about them. If you use it, we store what you changed — and only what you changed, not a fresh copy of the whole record — together with your name and email, any note or tier query you write, and, if you dragged your map pin, the coordinates you moved it from and to. Your pin and your address line are published on your listing; the page says so before you edit them, and if you work from home you are welcome to leave the pin on your village or nearest town rather than your door. Nothing you send changes the map by itself — we read every submission before anything moves.

About that link. It is a convenience, not a login. Anyone holding it can open and edit that one listing, so treat it like any other link in your inbox, and tell us if it goes astray and we will issue a new one. It gives access to nothing else.

Where we got your email address. To send those links we collected contact addresses that businesses publish on their own websites. If yours is one of them and you would rather we did not hold it, tell us and we will delete it — and take the listing down as well, if that is what you want.

When you email us. We keep the correspondence so we have a record of corrections, right-of-reply requests and evidence about materials.

When you use search. Search runs partly through Google’s Gemini API, which interprets what you typed so we can match it to listings. Your search text is sent to Google for that purpose. It isn’t stored against you or linked to an account — but please don’t type anything personal into a search box, here or anywhere else.

When you simply visit. Our host keeps standard server logs — IP address, browser type, the page requested, the time — for security and reliability. We don’t use them to build a picture of you.

What we don’t do

No advertising. No analytics or tracking pixels. No cookies set by us. No profiling, no selling or sharing of data with anyone for marketing, and no mailing list you didn’t ask to join.

The map does store a couple of things in your own browser’s storage — whether you’ve already seen the introduction, and an API key if you have chosen to enter one. That stays on your device and never reaches us.

Who else handles it

Running a website means other companies see some data on our behalf, mostly because your browser talks to them directly:

  • Vercel — hosting, server logs, and the private store our two review queues sit in.
  • Google — the Gemini API that interprets search queries, and Google Fonts, which your browser fetches to render the type on every page.
  • postcodes.io — when a business moves its map pin we send those coordinates, and nothing else, to work out which county the listing belongs in.
  • CARTO and unpkg — the map’s background tiles and mapping library, on the main map and on the pin map in the update page. Requesting them reveals your IP address to those services, as it would on any site.

Suggestions sent before September 2026 reached us by email through Formspree, and those older messages still sit in our inbox. The form no longer uses it.

Some of these are based outside the UK, so information may be processed abroad. We rely on the transfer protections in their standard terms, and we send them no more than the task needs.

Our legal basis

For running and securing the site, and for researching and publishing listings, our basis is legitimate interests — there is a public interest in an independent, unpaid record of what is actually made and grown here. The same basis covers holding a business’s published contact address so we can write to it once about its own listing. You can object to that at any time, and we will stop.

Where you volunteer your own contact details — in the suggestion form, or on your listing’s update page — we rely on your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.

How long we keep it

Suggestions, listing updates and correspondence are kept while they remain part of the evidence trail behind a listing, so we can show why a business is tiered the way it is and what it told us itself. A suggestion we decide not to list is kept as a short note of the decision, so the same business arriving again does not have to be researched twice.

Ask us to delete your personal details from any of that and we will, keeping only the underlying evidence about the business itself.

Listings and personal information

Almost everything on the map is public information about a business. Where a business trades under someone’s own name — a single-handed potter or jeweller, say — that record may also count as personal information. If you’re that person and you want a detail corrected, or the listing removed altogether, just tell us. We don’t argue about it.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, object to what we’re doing with it, or ask us to restrict it. Email hello@grownandmade.uk and we’ll respond within a month.

If you think we’ve handled your information badly, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We’d rather you told us first so we can put it right.

Changes

If this policy changes we’ll update the date at the top. Anything significant will be flagged on the page rather than slipped in quietly.
