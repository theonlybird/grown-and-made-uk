Terms of use

Grown and Made UK was built out of frustration at how difficult it was to find great UK businesses. The result is an independent editorial map of fantastic businesses we have found. Grown and Made UK is not a shop and we are not an official certification scheme. All we do is publish our honest assessment from the evidence we can find, usually on websites. We will always look to keep the information on this website as up to date as possible and correct ourselves when we are wrong. Crucially, nobody pays us to feature on the website and nobody can buy Gold or Silver tiers.

What the map is

It’s a directory. We’re not a marketplace, so no transactions take place on this website. All we do is point you in the direction of the growers and makers, and you deal with them directly. We have no commercial relationship with the businesses listed, and being listed doesn’t mean a business has agreed to anything, or even knows we exist.

Gold and Silver are our opinion

A tier is our editorial assessment, formed in good faith from the evidence publicly available to us at the time, against the published standard. It is not a certification, an audit, a guarantee or a warranty, and it isn’t a statement about quality, ethics, environmental or employment practice. If we cannot find any clear evidence as to the origin of the main material, a business gets Silver until we can — that reflects the state of the evidence, not a judgement about the business.

Of course supply chains do change, and sometimes quietly. A listing that was accurate when we researched it may not be accurate today. If a detail matters to your purchase, please do ask the business.

If you’re a listed business

You have a standing right of reply. If you think a listing, a tier or an evidence note is wrong, please tell us with whatever evidence you have and we’ll review it. If you would like to be removed from the Grown and Made UK website entirely, please do let us know by email and we will do so promptly.

Nobody pays

No business can pay to be listed, promoted, re-ordered or upgraded. We carry no advertising and take no commission on anything you buy. We were created to be an independent directory and will remain that way.
